# Spear Helper
> Stop missing your lunge swaps in the middle of a fight!

## What it does

- Automatically swaps to your lunge spear when you hold the keybind
- Swaps back after the lunge so you don't get stuck on cooldown
- Works in fast-paced PvP where every millisecond matters

## Requirements

- Java 21+
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- Fabric Loader 0.18.6+
- Lunge enchantment on your spear (Lunge III recommended)

## How to use

1. Bind your key in **Options > Controls > Key Binds > Spear Helper > Swap Spear**
2. Make sure your spear has the Lunge enchantment
3. Select a slot with low or no cooldown before activating

   ✅ Good slots: bread, golden carrot, wind charge, ender pearl  
   ❌ Bad slots: sword, mace, axe, pickaxe

4. Hold the keybind for continuous lunging, or press once for single use
5. Make sure you have enough hunger/saturation!

> ⚠️ Warning: this mod may be considered a cheat or macro on some servers. Use at your own risk.
